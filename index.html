About

I work in the research department at FlashLabs, where I co-lead Chroma, a real-time end-to-end spoken dialogue model that reasons over what it hears. My interests sit at the intersection of speech, language, and reasoning, with ongoing work on retrieval-augmented LLMs (IndexRAG) and on how upstream speech-recognition errors propagate through multi-hop retrieval. I completed MSc degrees in CS (AI) and Internet & Web-based Systems at TU Darmstadt.

Earlier work explored multi-agent reinforcement learning with graph neural networks, specifically generalization across unseen network topologies, and test-time adaptation for EEG foundation models.

  • Spoken dialogue. End-to-end models that hold a real-time conversation and reason over speech.
  • Retrieval-augmented generation. Architectures that pre-compute cross-document inference at index time.
  • Robustness. How errors introduced upstream by speech recognition are amplified rather than absorbed as a question travels through multi-hop retrieval.
